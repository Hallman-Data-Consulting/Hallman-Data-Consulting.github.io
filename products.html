The demo runs on Maple Street Gifts, a fictitious UK gift retailer with two years of real order history. No sign-in, nothing to install. Break it however you like — the data resets from a clean copy every night.

  • Revenue, product, customer, and country views in one place
  • Add, edit, and delete orders directly, with every entry checked before it saves
  • Every change shows up in the numbers immediately
